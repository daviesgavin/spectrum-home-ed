1. Who we are

Spectrum Home Ed (spectrumhomeed.com) is a home education service for families of autistic, nonverbal and other special-needs learners. It is run by Gavin Davies in Western Australia. In this policy, "we" and "us" means Spectrum Home Ed, and "you" means the parent or carer using it.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). We have chosen to follow them even where the law may not strictly require a business of our size to.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Your account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed), so even we can't read them.
  • Child profiles: your child's first name, birth year, state, support level, how they communicate, their interests, sensory notes, your goals for them, and their learning time (plus your reason, if you reduce it).
  • Plans and records: the lesson plans we write for you, any changes you make, how each lesson went, and your notes.
  • Photos and videos: any evidence you choose to add to a lesson.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email.

Information from payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe tells us your email address, billing city, state and country, which plan you chose, and whether your subscription is active.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the website, we count the visit. We record the page, the website that sent you (if any), your device type (phone, tablet or computer), your browser language, and your approximate city and country, which we work out from your device's time zone. To count unique visitors, we keep a random ID in your browser's storage. We do not store your IP address and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Signing in keeps a login token in your browser's storage so you stay signed in. The phrase board also keeps its settings in your browser's storage.

3. Children's information

Spectrum Home Ed is for parents and carers aged 18 or over. Children don't have their own accounts. Information about your child is added by you, as their parent or carer, and by using the service you confirm you have the authority to provide it.

Some of what you tell us, such as your child's support needs, diagnosis-related details, communication and sensory needs, may be sensitive information under the Privacy Act. We collect it only because you choose to provide it, and only so the lesson plans suit your child. Only your child's first name, state and support level are needed. Everything else is optional, and a first name or nickname is fine.

Photos and videos: the first time you add a photo or video of a child, we ask for your consent and record it. Photos are shrunk on your phone before upload, which also removes the location data that phones store inside photos.

Spectrum Talk phrase board: phrases, photos, scripts, tap counts and sign videos stay on the device you use the board on. They are not sent to us unless you save a backup file and choose to share it yourself.

We don't use children's information for marketing, profiling or advertising, and we never sell it.

4. How we use your information

  • To run your account and sign you in.
  • To write lesson plans that suit your child, and show how they cover your state's learning areas.
  • To store your records, notes, photos and videos so you can use them as evidence for your home education moderator.
  • To take payments and manage your subscription.
  • To send emails you need, such as confirming your account or resetting your password. We don't send marketing emails without asking first.
  • To understand, in total, how many people visit and where from, so we can improve the service.
  • To keep the service secure, and to meet our legal obligations.

6. Where it's stored

Your account, child profiles, plans, records, photos and videos are stored with Supabase in its Sydney, Australia data centre. Photos and videos are kept in private storage. They are never given public web addresses: each time you view one, the app creates a link that only works for a short time.

7. Keeping it safe

  • Everything is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Database rules mean each family can only read and change their own records, photos and videos.
  • Passwords are hashed, and card details are handled only by Stripe.
  • Admin access is limited to a short list of people.

No online service is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

8. How long we keep it

  • We keep your information while your account is open.
  • Things you delete are removed straight away. Deleted database records can stay in our automatic backups for up to 7 days before they are gone for good.
  • Payment records may be kept for as long as tax law requires, which is usually 5 years.
  • Visit counts don't identify you. We may keep them for as long as they are useful.

9. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: you can see and edit everything about your child in the app. You can also ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Delete: you can delete any photo, video, plan or child profile in the app. Deleting a child's profile also deletes their plans, records, photos and videos. To delete your whole account, email us and we'll do it within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent: you can stop adding photos at any time and delete the ones you've added.
  • Visit counting: clearing your browser's storage resets the random visitor ID.

If your child is old enough to have a view about their own information, we encourage you to include them in these decisions.

10. If something goes wrong

If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell the affected families as soon as we can, explain what happened and what to do, and notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where required.

11. Questions and complaints

Email hello@spectrumhomeed.com. We aim to reply within 7 days, and to resolve complaints within 30 days.

If you're not happy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes or the law changes. We'll show the new date at the top, and if a change affects how we use your child's information, we'll tell you by email or in the app first.
