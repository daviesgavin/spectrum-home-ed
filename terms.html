3. What the service is, and isn't

Spectrum Home Ed is a planning and record-keeping tool. It suggests lessons and activities, tracks learning time, and stores your records and evidence.

It is not:

  • medical, therapy, behavioural or allied health advice, or a replacement for your child's doctors and therapists;
  • legal advice about home education, school, disability or NDIS matters;
  • connected to, or endorsed by, any education department, curriculum authority or the NDIS.

Information about state requirements is a guide only. Rules change, so check with your state's home education authority.

4. Safety

Many activities happen in real life: swimming, cooking, outings, shopping and movement. You must:

  • supervise your child at all times, and especially around water, heat, sharp tools, roads and crowds;
  • check every food activity against your child's allergies, intolerances, diet and swallowing needs;
  • adjust or skip anything that doesn't suit your child's age, size, abilities, sensory needs or health;
  • follow any advice from your child's doctors and therapists over anything in a plan.

If an activity doesn't feel safe for your child, don't do it.

5. AI-written plans

Lesson plans are written by artificial intelligence, based on what you tell us about your child. AI can make mistakes, misunderstand, or suggest something that isn't right for your child. Read each plan before you use it, and change or remove anything that doesn't fit. You can edit every lesson in the app.
